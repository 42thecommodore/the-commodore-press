# About the Press

The Commodore Press is a working library in {{W_WINGS}} wings: {{N_BOOKS}} titles on the ideas people spent their lives inside, {{N_LIVES}} lives, and an Atlas of {{N_PEOPLE}} people the house listens to now. Where the people who know most still disagree, the disagreement is printed in the entry instead of smoothed over.

## What the house promises

- Every figure carries a named source, and a source means a document somebody opened.
- Disputes are printed under *Where it is contested*, stated at their strongest.
- Corrections are appended in the colophon, numbered, and never quietly patched.
- Lines credited "after" someone are compressed notes, not quotations.
- The site is built with AI assistance, and says so.

These cost something. A number the house cannot source does not ship, even when it is probably right, and the corrections list is a public record of every time the house was wrong.
